    How to play

    The goal

    Two players command opposing armies on a hex map. Win by capturing the enemy capital (★), or by holding enough victory-point cities when the last turn ends. The panel under the map shows the score and what each side needs.

    Reading a counter

    1. The three numbers are attack – defense – movement. A 6-3-6 armor unit hits hard, defends weakly and moves far.
    2. The symbol shows the unit type: ✕ infantry, oval armor, oval with ✕ mechanized, dot artillery.
    3. A light corner tab means the unit is reduced (one more loss destroys it). A red S means it is out of supply and fights and moves at half strength.

    Your turn, step by step

    1. Move. Tap one of your units. Lit hexes show where it can go; tap one to move there. Each unit moves once. Roads are fast; forest, hills and marsh are slow. A unit that moves next to an enemy must stop there.
    2. Made a mistake? Undo move takes back moves until you start fighting.
    3. Tap Go to combat when you have finished moving.
    4. Attack. Enemy units you can reach are outlined in red. Tap one. Your neighbouring units join the attack automatically; tap any of them to leave it out. Artillery can join from two hexes away.
    5. Check the odds in the panel. Higher odds (3:1 and up) usually win; forests, hills, cities and rivers make the defender tougher. Then tap Roll the die.
    6. If the defender is destroyed or retreats, you may move one attacker into the empty hex. Tap the glowing unit, or choose Stay put.
    7. Each enemy hex can be attacked once and each of your units attacks once. When you are done, tap End turn.

    Handing over

    1. Same device: pass the phone to the other player and they tap Start turn.
    2. By email: choose Send turn by email, copy the text and email it. Your opponent opens Hexfront, chooses Load turn from the menu and pastes it in. Arrows on the map show what you did.

    Tips

    1. Gang up: two or three units on one defender give much better odds than attacking alone.
    2. Keep a path back to your home edge or a supply city. Units cut off by enemy units or their zones of control lose half their strength.
    3. Defend in forests, hills and cities. Attack in the open.
    4. Turn on Enemy ZOC to see where enemy units will stop your movement, and Supply to see your supply network.
    5. Drag to pan the map and pinch (or use + and −) to zoom.

    Choose a front

    Every scenario uses the same rules. Units change colour per front, and some fronts add special units.
