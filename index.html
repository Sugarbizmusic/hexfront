    How to play

    The goal

    Two armies fight over a hex map. Win by capturing the enemy capital (★), or by holding enough victory-point cities when the last turn ends. The Objectives panel shows the score and what each side needs.

    Reading a counter

    1. The name is the formation (zoom in to see it). The three numbers are attack – defense – movement.
    2. The symbol shows the type: ✕ infantry, oval armor, oval with ✕ mechanized, dot artillery.
    3. A light corner tab means reduced (one more loss destroys it). A red S means out of supply. A sandbag badge means entrenched.
    4. Tap Info in the toolbar, then any unit, to see its full stats for the ground it stands on.

    Your turn, step by step

    1. Move. Tap one of your units, then a lit hex. Roads are fast; forest, hills and marsh are slow. A unit that moves next to an enemy must stop. Next jumps to a unit that has not moved; Undo takes a move back.
    2. Orders. Tap Orders, then a unit, to Entrench instead of moving. If it is not attacked before your next turn it digs in and becomes one column harder to attack until it moves.
    3. Tap Go to combat when you have finished moving.
    4. Attack. Enemy units you can reach are outlined in red. Tap one; the Battle panel opens with the odds. Your units join automatically; tap them in the list to leave them out. Then Roll the die.
    5. Bombard. Artillery can fire on its own from two hexes away. Leave only artillery in the attack: it cannot be hurt, the target cannot be forced back, but a hit costs the defender a step. You can still assault the same unit afterwards.
    6. If the defender is destroyed or retreats, you may move one attacker into the empty hex. Then tap End turn.

    Fog of war

    If fog is switched on for a game, you only see 3 hexes from your units (5 for recon). Grey hexes are unseen; faded counters show where an enemy was last spotted. The computer plays under the same fog.

    Panels

    The bottom bar opens Battle (odds and results), Objectives (score, cities, scenario rules), Forces (all your units: tap one to find it), Log (what just happened) and Charts (terrain and combat tables). Tap outside a panel or its button again to close it.

    Tips

    1. Two or three units on one defender give far better odds than attacking alone. Soften tough targets with artillery first.
    2. Keep a path back to your home edge or a supply city. Cut-off units fight at half strength.
    3. Defend in forests, hills and cities, and entrench units that will hold a line. Attack in the open.

    Choose a front

    Every scenario uses the same rules. Units change colour per front, and some fronts add special units.
